Academic Prototype & Medical Safety Notice

RetinaAI is an academic deep-learning prototype intended for research and educational demonstration. Model outputs are screening results and should not be considered a medical diagnosis. Predictions and Grad-CAM visualizations should be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional.
